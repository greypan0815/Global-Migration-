## Scope and limitations

The original material covers 36 European countries and additional global destinations, often using Mainland China documentation and comparisons. The Taiwan-specific additions are a **first-pass overlay**, with six destination-level route families checked against official government sources on **October 30, 2026**. They do **not** constitute a passport-by-passport audit of every original route.

**Do not apply Mainland-specific exclusions, apostille/document procedures, dual-citizenship claims, cost-of-living baselines, or visa-bulletin interpretations to Taiwanese applicants without independent verification.** Visa eligibility, live application availability, employment rights, permanent residency and citizenship are separate questions.
